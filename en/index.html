Guyun

Your files, in your own bucket

Connect your own object storage — Alibaba Cloud OSS, Tencent COS, Cloudflare R2, Backblaze B2, MinIO, or any S3-compatible service. No accounts, and the keys stay in this device's keychain. Files go from the device straight to your bucket, with no server of mine in between.

  • iOS 17 / iPadOS 17 / macOS 14 and later
  • Since 1.5, connect your own AI client
  • App is free; storage is billed by your provider
  • No relay server — files go straight to your bucket

Fengyu 风雨如磐

A base map for modern China

Territory, institutions, people, famines, newspapers and railways from 1840 to 1949, laid on one map you can flip year by year. Ceded areas appear on the year they were signed; themed threads read like essays; every entry carries its sources. It does not tell the story for you — it lays the material out.

  • 1,532 entries, 736 sources, 23 themes
  • Qing territory settled; late Qing in progress
  • Republic era only just begun
  • Borders are reconstruction, not survey

Approach

One less server layer, wherever it is possible.

  1. 01

    Local first

    Requests go from this device straight to your bucket — there is no server of mine in between. Where the bucket lives, and which provider runs it, is your call.

  2. 02

    Hands off your data

    No accounts, no telemetry, no servers of ours; the keys stay in this device's keychain.

  3. 03

    Free to use

    The app itself costs nothing. The only bill is your own storage, at your provider's list price — it never passes through me.
